# Privacy Policy – AI Game Filter for Steam Store

_Last updated: 9 November 2026 (v1.2.0)

AI Game Filter for Steam Store ("the extension") does not collect, store on external servers, sell or share any personal data.

**What the extension does with data**
- It runs only on `https://store.steampowered.com/*` and on the games page of Steam Community profiles (`https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/*/games*`, `https://steamcommunity.com/id/*/games*`).
- To check whether a game carries an AI-generated content disclosure, it requests that game's public store page from store.steampowered.com (the same site you are browsing) and reads it locally in your browser.
- - When you are logged in to the Steam store, the extension reads your public Steam ID from the page and keeps it locally, only to build the link to your own library page.
- Results (game ID, yes/no, timestamp), your Steam ID and your settings (on/off, highlight, hide, library marking) are saved locally with `chrome.storage.local` on your device. You can erase them at any time with the "Clear cache" button or by removing the extension.

**What it does not do**
- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or remote code.
- It does not read your Steam account details, purchases or credentials.
